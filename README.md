# Privacy Policy for Plotato - Garden Planner

**Effective date:** November 18, 2026

Plotato - Garden Planner ("Plotato", "the app") is a garden planning app for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Summary

- Plotato does **not** require an account.
- Plotato does **not** sell your information or use it for advertising.
- Plotato does **not** use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
- Your garden data stays on your device.
- The only information that can leave your device is the ZIP code you enter, which may be sent to a ZIP code lookup service (see below).

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- Your garden plants, planting dates, tasks, and notes
- Square-foot garden layouts
- Seed inventory and custom varieties
- Your location settings (ZIP code and frost dates) and app preferences
- A debug log of app activity, which you can view and clear in Settings

We (the developer) cannot access this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

### Android backup

If you have turned on Android device backup, Android may include the app's data in your backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and you control it in your device settings. The developer cannot access your backups.

## ZIP code lookup

Plotato asks for your U.S. ZIP code to estimate your frost dates and growing zone. Frost dates come from a table built into the app, so this part works offline.

If the built-in table doesn't have a town name for your ZIP code, the app sends **only that ZIP code** over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the free third-party service [Zippopotam.us](https://www.zippopotam.us/) to look up the town name. A ZIP code indicates your approximate location. Like any web server, that service can also see your device's IP address when the request is made. Zippopotam.us handles requests under its own practices, which we do not control. The app does not send any other information, and the lookup is skipped if you are offline.

We (the developer) do not receive or store your ZIP code. It is saved only on your device.

## Calendar access (optional)

If you choose to export tasks to your calendar, the app asks for permission to read and write your calendars. The app uses this permission only to:

- List your calendars so you can choose one
- Add your garden tasks as events, and find events it added before so it doesn't create duplicates

The app does not read, upload, or share your other calendar events. You can revoke this permission at any time in Android Settings. If you add a single task through your calendar app's editor instead, that calendar app saves the event.

## Notifications (optional)

The app can show reminders for your garden tasks. The app schedules these on your device; they are not sent from a server. You can turn notifications off in the app or in Android Settings.

## Data security

- Your Plotato data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access.
- The ZIP code lookup described above uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
- The app has no account system and does not send your garden data to any server, so there is no copy of it held by us that could be exposed.

## Data retention

- Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
- Calendar events you export stay in your calendar until you delete them in your calendar app.
- Android backups are kept by Google according to your Google account and device backup settings.
- We (the developer) do not keep any of your data.

## Deleting your data

All of your Plotato data is stored on your device. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Because we never receive your data, we have no copy to delete.

## Children's privacy

Plotato is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
